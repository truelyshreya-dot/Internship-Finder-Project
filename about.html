About Internship Finder

What is Internship Finder?

Internship Finder is a trusted online platform designed to connect college students with verified companies offering internship opportunities. The platform ensures transparency, authenticity, and skill-based matching for meaningful career growth.

For Students

  • Register using verified college credentials
  • Explore internships based on skills and interests
  • Apply directly to companies
  • Track application status
  • Build and update resumes anytime

For Companies

  • Register using official business email and GST details
  • Post internship opportunities
  • View and shortlist suitable candidates
  • Ensure genuine hiring through verified student data

Why Choose Internship Finder?

  • Verified students and companies
  • No fake postings or profiles
  • Skill-based internship matching
  • User-friendly and secure system
  • Designed for academic and professional growth

How It Works

Students register and list their skills. Companies post internships with required skills. The platform matches both sides efficiently, helping students gain real-world experience and companies find the right talent.
